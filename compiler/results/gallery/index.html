Every pgfplots figure under results/, generated by pysimcount.figgallery at e5d9ee6. Filters combine with AND; the formula filter keeps figures that show every ticked formula. Research-question relevance is a hand-written tagging (gallery/rq-relevance.json), not read from the figures: key figures go in the section at the top, related ones below. The main-body selection (gallery/main-body.json, a proposal; rationale in gallery/main-body-selection.md) is shown first, and its figures are outlined wherever they appear. Where Lie-Trotter S1 took a third or more of a figure's y-axis, the figure is shown as a copy without it (pysimcount.figdrop; list in gallery/lie-trotter-removed.md), linked to the original.
